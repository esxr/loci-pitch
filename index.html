Why It Works

Backed by neuroscience.
Not just gamification.

The Method of Loci activates the hippocampus — the brain's spatial navigation and memory formation centre.

Spatial Memory Advantage

+62%
Participants trained with Method of Loci recalled 62% more words than control group after just 6 weeks of training.
Long-Term Retention

4 mo+
Memory champions using spatial techniques show superior recall even after 4+ months without review.
Hippocampal Activation

Spatial navigation activates place cells and grid cells — the brain's native "GPS" for encoding location-based memory.
