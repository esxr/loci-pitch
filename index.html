Science
Why It Works

Backed by neuroscience.
Not just gamification.

The Method of Loci activates the hippocampus — the brain's spatial navigation and memory formation centre. Loci automates the hardest part: building the palace itself.

Spatial Memory Advantage
Dresler et al. 2017 · Neuron
Significant
Participants trained in the Method of Loci showed dramatically higher word recall than control groups — after just weeks of practice.
Long-Term Retention
Maguire et al. 2003 · Nature Neuroscience
Months
Memory champions using spatial techniques show superior recall months later — without additional review or practice.
Place Cells & Grid Cells
O'Keefe & Moser · 2014 Nobel Prize
Nobel
The 2014 Nobel Prize in Medicine confirmed the brain has a built-in GPS — place cells and grid cells that encode location as memory.
Multimedia Learning Theory
Mayer 2009 · Cambridge University Press
3-in-1
Combining visual, spatial, and verbal channels produces the strongest memory traces. Loci activates all three simultaneously.
TRIPLE ENCODING — SPATIAL + VISUAL + SEMANTIC
KNOWLEDGE GRAPH TO 3D SPATIAL MEMORY
WHY LOCI WORKS FOR STUDENTS —The brain encodes spatial information effortlessly — students remember every game map they've ever played. Loci harnesses this by combining three memory systems at once: spatial (exploring rooms), visual (3D artifacts), and verbal (conversations with AI guides). This is how the brain wants to learn.
